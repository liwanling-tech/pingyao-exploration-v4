pingyao-osm.json contains the distributed filtered geographic database under
ODbL, including selected OSM IDs, retained tags, geometry, bounds, and all six
landmark reference points. Usernames, changesets, business contact details and
unneeded POIs are not retained. No coordinates were copied from commercial maps.

pingyao-plan.svg is a rendered map from that data, with simplified styling.
Its actual street and wall geometry is retained; it is not an AI illustration,
an official survey, or a turn-by-turn navigation map. Route overlays represent
study order, not traversable paths. Missing building footprints are not invented.

Rebuild from an equivalent Overpass JSON response:
node scripts/build-pingyao-map.mjs output/pingyao-osm.json
